Introduction · Introducción

Transportation vocabulary helps you travel, ask for directions, and talk about how you get to work or school every day.

El vocabulario de transporte te ayuda a viajar, pedir direcciones, y hablar de cómo llegas al trabajo o escuela cada día.

Did you know? / ¿Sabías qué?

English uses "by" for most transportation: "by car," "by bus," "by plane" — but "on foot" for walking.

El inglés usa "by" para la mayoría de los transportes: "by car," "by bus," "by plane" — pero "on foot" para caminar.

Pronunciation · Pronunciación
by /baɪ/
luggage /ˈlʌɡɪdʒ/

Don't confuse "by" (transportation) with "buy" (purchase) or "bye" (goodbye) — all three sound identical. / No confundas "by" (transporte) con "buy" (comprar) o "bye" (adiós) — los tres suenan idénticos.

"I go to work with bus"
"I go to work by bus"
6
Grammar · Preposition BY with transport

Use "by" + means of transport with no article: "by car," "by bus," "by plane." Exception: "on foot" for walking.

Usa "by" + medio de transporte sin artículo: "by car," "by bus," "by plane." Excepción: "on foot" para caminar.

Tip · Consejo del profesor

Study Tip / Consejo de estudio

Every time you travel this week, say out loud in English how you're traveling — 'I am going by...' — to build the habit.
