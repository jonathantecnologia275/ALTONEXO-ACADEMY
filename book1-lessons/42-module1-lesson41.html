Introduction · Introducción

Telling time correctly helps you catch buses, arrive on time for appointments, and follow daily schedules.

Decir la hora correctamente te ayuda a tomar buses, llegar a tiempo a citas, y seguir horarios diarios.

Did you know? / ¿Sabías qué?

Native English speakers often say "half past three" instead of "three thirty" — both are correct.

Los hablantes nativos de inglés a menudo dicen "half past three" en vez de "three thirty" — ambos son correctos.

Pronunciation · Pronunciación
o'clock /əˈklɒk/
quarter /ˈkwɔːrtər/

"O'clock" is only used with exact hours: "three o'clock," never "three thirty o'clock." / "O'clock" solo se usa con horas exactas: "three o'clock," nunca "three thirty o'clock."

"What hour is it?"
"What time is it?"
6
Grammar · Time expressions

To ask the time, say "What time is it?" To answer, use "It's + time": "It's ten o'clock," "It's half past six," "It's a quarter to nine."

Para preguntar la hora, di "What time is it?" Para responder, usa "It's + hora": "It's ten o'clock," "It's half past six," "It's a quarter to nine."
