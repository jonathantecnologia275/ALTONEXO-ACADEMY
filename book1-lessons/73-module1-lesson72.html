Introduction · Introducción

Modal verbs are special helping verbs that add meaning like ability, permission, obligation, advice, or possibility to a main verb. They never change form (no -s, no -ing, no -ed) and are always followed by a base verb.

Los verbos modales son verbos auxiliares especiales que agregan significado como habilidad, permiso, obligación, consejo, o posibilidad a un verbo principal. Nunca cambian de forma (sin -s, sin -ing, sin -ed) y siempre van seguidos de un verbo base.

Did you know? / ¿Sabías qué?

Modal verbs are famous for never changing form: you always say "she can," never "she cans" — this makes them some of the easiest verbs to use once you memorize the rule.

Los verbos modales son famosos por nunca cambiar de forma: siempre dices "she can," nunca "she cans" — esto los hace de los verbos más fáciles de usar una vez que memorizas la regla.

Pronunciation · Pronunciación
mustn't /ˈmʌsənt/ (silent t)
could /kʊd/

"Mustn't" has a silent "t" — it's pronounced "MUSS-uhnt," and it means prohibition (not allowed), which is very different from "don't have to" (not necessary, but allowed). / "Mustn't" tiene una "t" muda — se pronuncia "MUSS-uhnt," y significa prohibición (no permitido), lo cual es muy diferente de "don't have to" (no es necesario, pero sí permitido).

"You don't must smoke here" (incorrect negative form)
"You mustn't smoke here" (mustn't = prohibited) OR "You don't have to smoke" (different meaning: not necessary)
6
Grammar · Must vs Should vs Mustn't vs Don't have to

MUST = strong obligation (a rule). SHOULD = advice/recommendation (not a rule). MUSTN'T = prohibition (not allowed at all). DON'T HAVE TO = no obligation (allowed, but not necessary). These four are easy to confuse but mean very different things.

MUST = obligación fuerte (una regla). SHOULD = consejo/recomendación (no es una regla). MUSTN'T = prohibición (no permitido en absoluto). DON'T HAVE TO = sin obligación (permitido, pero no necesario). Estos cuatro son fáciles de confundir pero significan cosas muy diferentes.

Tip · Consejo del profesor

Study Tip / Consejo de estudio

Remember: modal verbs never change form and are always followed by the base verb without 'to' (except 'have to' and 'ought to') — 'she must go,' never 'she musts to go.'

Recuerda: los verbos modales nunca cambian de forma y siempre van seguidos del verbo base sin 'to' (excepto 'have to' y 'ought to') — 'she must go,' nunca 'she musts to go.'
