Introduction · Introducción

Conditional sentences describe cause and effect: "if X happens, then Y happens." English has 4 main types, each used for a different level of reality or possibility, from general truths to impossible past situations.

Las oraciones condicionales describen causa y efecto: "si pasa X, entonces pasa Y." El inglés tiene 4 tipos principales, cada uno usado para un nivel diferente de realidad o posibilidad, desde verdades generales hasta situaciones pasadas imposibles.

Did you know? / ¿Sabías qué?

The Third Conditional talks about an imaginary past that never happened: "If I had studied, I would have passed" — meaning the person did NOT study and did NOT pass.

El Tercer Condicional habla de un pasado imaginario que nunca sucedió: "If I had studied, I would have passed" — significa que la persona NO estudió y NO aprobó.

In the Second Conditional, English traditionally uses "were" for all subjects (even "I" and "he/she"): "If I were you..." — not "If I was you." / En el Segundo Condicional, el inglés tradicionalmente usa "were" para todos los sujetos (incluso "I" y "he/she"): "If I were you..." — no "If I was you."

Grammar · The 4 conditional types at a glance

Zero: general truths (if + present, present). First: real future possibility (if + present, will). Second: unlikely/hypothetical present (if + past simple, would). Third: impossible past (if + past perfect, would have + participle).

Cero: verdades generales (if + presente, presente). Primero: posibilidad real futura (if + presente, will). Segundo: hipotético/poco probable en presente (if + pasado simple, would). Tercero: pasado imposible (if + pasado perfecto, would have + participio).

Tip · Consejo del profesor

Study Tip / Consejo de estudio

Never use 'will' inside the if-clause itself (not "if it will rain") — 'will' only belongs in the result clause: "If it rains, I will stay home."

Nunca uses 'will' dentro de la cláusula con if (no "if it will rain") — 'will' solo pertenece en la cláusula de resultado: "If it rains, I will stay home."
