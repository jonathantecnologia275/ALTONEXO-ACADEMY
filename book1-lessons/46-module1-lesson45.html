Introduction · Introducción

Talking about professions lets you describe your work, ask about others' jobs, and understand career-related conversations.

Hablar de profesiones te permite describir tu trabajo, preguntar sobre el trabajo de otros, y entender conversaciones relacionadas con carreras.

Did you know? / ¿Sabías qué?

English always uses "a" or "an" before a job: "I am a teacher," "She is an engineer" — never "I am teacher."

El inglés siempre usa "a" o "an" antes de una profesión: "I am a teacher," "She is an engineer" — nunca "I am teacher."

Pronunciation · Pronunciación
engineer /ˌɛndʒɪˈnɪər/
a vs an

Use "an" before words starting with a vowel sound: "an engineer," "an artist." Use "a" before consonant sounds: "a teacher," "a doctor." / Usa "an" antes de palabras que empiezan con sonido vocal: "an engineer," "an artist." Usa "a" antes de sonidos consonantes: "a teacher," "a doctor."

"She is doctor"
"She is a doctor"
6
Grammar · Articles A/AN with jobs

Always use "a" or "an" before a profession: "He is a mechanic," "I am an engineer." Choose "an" only before a vowel sound.

Siempre usa "a" o "an" antes de una profesión: "He is a mechanic," "I am an engineer." Elige "an" solo antes de un sonido vocal.
