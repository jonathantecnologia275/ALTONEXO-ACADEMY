Introduction · Introducción

Learning house vocabulary helps you describe where you live, give directions inside a home, and talk about furniture and rooms.

Aprender el vocabulario de la casa te ayuda a describir dónde vives, dar direcciones dentro de una casa, y hablar de muebles y habitaciones.

Did you know? / ¿Sabías qué?

"There is" is for singular things ("There is a bed"), while "There are" is for plural things ("There are two chairs").

"There is" es para cosas singulares ("There is a bed"), mientras que "There are" es para cosas plurales ("There are two chairs").

Pronunciation · Pronunciación
there is / there are
furniture /ˈfɜːrnɪtʃər/

Don't confuse "there," "their," and "they're" — they sound the same but mean different things. / No confundas "there," "their," y "they're" — suenan igual pero significan cosas diferentes.

"In my house have three bedrooms"
"There are three bedrooms in my house"
6
Grammar · THERE IS / THERE ARE

Use "There is" + singular noun and "There are" + plural noun to describe what exists in a place: "There is a kitchen. There are two bathrooms."

Usa "There is" + sustantivo singular y "There are" + sustantivo plural para describir qué existe en un lugar: "There is a kitchen. There are two bathrooms."
