Introduction · Introducción

Beyond FANBOYS, English has correlative conjunctions (working in pairs) and subordinating conjunctions (connecting a main clause to a dependent one). Together, they let you express contrast, condition, reason, and time relationships with precision.

Más allá de FANBOYS, el inglés tiene conjunciones correlativas (que funcionan en pares) y conjunciones subordinantes (que conectan una oración principal con una dependiente). Juntas, te permiten expresar contraste, condición, razón, y relaciones de tiempo con precisión.

Did you know? / ¿Sabías qué?

Subordinating conjunctions create a dependent clause that cannot stand alone: "Because it was raining" is NOT a complete sentence by itself — it needs a main clause attached.

Las conjunciones subordinantes crean una oración dependiente que no puede existir sola: "Because it was raining" NO es una oración completa por sí sola — necesita una oración principal adjunta.

Pronunciation · Pronunciación
although /ɔːlˈðoʊ/
unless /ʌnˈlɛs/

"Although" and "though" mean the same thing, but "though" can also go at the END of a sentence informally: "I like it. It's expensive, though." / "Although" y "though" significan lo mismo, pero "though" también puede ir al FINAL de una oración informalmente: "I like it. It's expensive, though."

"Although it was raining, but I went outside" (using both 'although' and 'but')
"Although it was raining, I went outside" (use only one connector, not both)
6
Grammar · Subordinating clause position

A subordinating clause can come first (with a comma after it) or second (no comma): "Because it was raining, I stayed home" = "I stayed home because it was raining." Never use both a subordinator AND a coordinator (like 'although...but') in the same sentence.

Una oración subordinada puede ir primero (con una coma después) o segundo (sin coma): "Because it was raining, I stayed home" = "I stayed home because it was raining." Nunca uses un subordinante Y un coordinante (como 'although...but') en la misma oración.

Tip · Consejo del profesor

Study Tip / Consejo de estudio

Never double up a subordinator with a coordinator (like 'although...but') — English only needs one connector to link the two ideas.

Nunca dupliques un subordinante con un coordinante (como 'although...but') — el inglés solo necesita un conector para unir las dos ideas.
