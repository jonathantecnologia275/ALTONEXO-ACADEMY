Introduction · Introducción

Adverbs add extra information to verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs. Adverbs of manner tell HOW, adverbs of place tell WHERE, and adverbs of time tell WHEN.

Los adverbios agregan información extra a verbos, adjetivos, u otros adverbios. Los adverbios de manera dicen CÓMO, los de lugar dicen DÓNDE, y los de tiempo dicen CUÁNDO.

Did you know? / ¿Sabías qué?

Most adverbs of manner are formed by adding "-ly" to an adjective: quick → quickly. But "good" becomes "well," and "fast" stays "fast" — a few common exceptions to remember.

La mayoría de los adverbios de manera se forman agregando "-ly" a un adjetivo: quick → quickly. Pero "good" se vuelve "well," y "fast" se queda "fast" — algunas excepciones comunes para recordar.

Pronunciation · Pronunciación
-ly /li/ ending
hardly ≠ hard

Be careful: "hardly" doesn't mean the same as "hard" — "hardly" means "almost not at all," while "hard" means "with a lot of effort." / Ten cuidado: "hardly" no significa lo mismo que "hard" — "hardly" significa "casi nada," mientras que "hard" significa "con mucho esfuerzo."

"She sings beautiful" / "I worked hardly today"
"She sings beautifully" (use the adverb form) / "I worked hard today" (not 'hardly,' which means the opposite)
6
Grammar · Adverb position rules

Manner adverbs usually go after the verb or object: "She speaks English fluently." Place adverbs usually go at the end: "I live here." Time adverbs are flexible but often go at the end or beginning: "I'll call you tomorrow" / "Tomorrow, I'll call you."

Los adverbios de manera generalmente van después del verbo u objeto: "She speaks English fluently." Los de lugar generalmente van al final: "I live here." Los de tiempo son flexibles pero a menudo van al final o al principio: "I'll call you tomorrow" / "Tomorrow, I'll call you."

Tip · Consejo del profesor

Study Tip / Consejo de estudio

When forming manner adverbs, always double-check irregular ones (good→well, fast→fast, hard→hard) — don't just add '-ly' automatically.

Al formar adverbios de manera, siempre verifica los irregulares (good→well, fast→fast, hard→hard) — no agregues '-ly' automáticamente.
