Introduction · Introducción

Knowing how to make a noun plural — and when NOT to — is essential for accurate, natural-sounding English.

Saber cómo hacer plural un sustantivo — y cuándo NO hacerlo — es esencial para un inglés preciso y natural.

Did you know? / ¿Sabías qué?

Some English nouns never take a plural "-s" even though they refer to many things: "information," "advice," and "furniture" are always singular.

Algunos sustantivos en inglés nunca llevan un "-s" plural aunque se refieran a muchas cosas: "information," "advice," y "furniture" siempre son singulares.

6
Grammar · Countable vs Uncountable

Countable nouns can be counted and have a plural form: "one apple, two apples." Uncountable nouns have no plural and use "some/much" instead of numbers: "some water," not "two waters."

Los sustantivos contables se pueden contar y tienen forma plural: "one apple, two apples." Los sustantivos incontables no tienen plural y usan "some/much" en vez de números: "some water," no "two waters."

Tip · Consejo del profesor

Study Tip / Consejo de estudio

When in doubt if a noun is countable, try putting a number in front of it: if 'two X's' sounds wrong, it's probably uncountable.

Cuando dudes si un sustantivo es contable, intenta poner un número delante: si 'two X's' suena mal, probablemente es incontable.
