Introduction · Introducción

Comparatives compare two things (bigger, more expensive), while superlatives compare three or more things, identifying the extreme (biggest, most expensive). The rule you use depends on the length of the adjective.

Los comparativos comparan dos cosas (bigger, more expensive), mientras que los superlativos comparan tres o más cosas, identificando el extremo (biggest, most expensive). La regla que uses depende de la longitud del adjetivo.

Did you know? / ¿Sabías qué?

For short adjectives (1 syllable), add "-er/-est": tall→taller→tallest. For long adjectives (3+ syllables), use "more/most": expensive→more expensive→most expensive.

Para adjetivos cortos (1 sílaba), agrega "-er/-est": tall→taller→tallest. Para adjetivos largos (3+ sílabas), usa "more/most": expensive→more expensive→most expensive.

Pronunciation · Pronunciación
-er /ər/
-est /ɪst/

Double the final consonant before "-er/-est" in short adjectives ending in one vowel + one consonant: big→bigger, hot→hotter. / Duplica la consonante final antes de "-er/-est" en adjetivos cortos que terminan en una vocal + una consonante: big→bigger, hot→hotter.

"This is more bigger than that" (double comparative)
"This is bigger than that" (use only one comparative form, never both -er AND more)
6
Grammar · Choosing -er/-est vs more/most

1 syllable: add -er/-est (tall/taller/tallest). 2 syllables ending in -y: change y to i + er/est (happy/happier/happiest). 2+ syllables (other): use more/most (careful/more careful/most careful). 3+ syllables: always more/most.

1 sílaba: agrega -er/-est (tall/taller/tallest). 2 sílabas terminadas en -y: cambia y por i + er/est (happy/happier/happiest). 2+ sílabas (otras): usa more/most (careful/more careful/most careful). 3+ sílabas: siempre more/most.

Tip · Consejo del profesor

Study Tip / Consejo de estudio

Memorize the irregular forms (good/better/best, bad/worse/worst) separately — they don't follow any pattern, so repetition is the only way to learn them.

Memoriza las formas irregulares (good/better/best, bad/worse/worst) por separado — no siguen ningún patrón, así que la repetición es la única forma de aprenderlas.
