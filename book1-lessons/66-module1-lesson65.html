Introduction · Introducción

Coordinating conjunctions connect words, phrases, or independent clauses of equal grammatical importance. English has exactly 7, remembered by the acronym FANBOYS: For, And, Nor, But, Or, Yet, So.

Las conjunciones de coordinación conectan palabras, frases, u oraciones independientes de igual importancia gramatical. El inglés tiene exactamente 7, recordadas con el acrónimo FANBOYS: For, And, Nor, But, Or, Yet, So.

Did you know? / ¿Sabías qué?

"FANBOYS" is a memory trick used in every English classroom worldwide: For, And, Nor, But, Or, Yet, So — the 7 coordinating conjunctions, and there are no others.

"FANBOYS" es un truco de memoria usado en cada salón de clases de inglés en el mundo: For, And, Nor, But, Or, Yet, So — las 7 conjunciones de coordinación, y no hay otras.

Pronunciation · Pronunciación
FANBOYS /ˈfænbɔɪz/
neither /ˈniːðər/ or /ˈnaɪðər/

"Neither" has two accepted pronunciations depending on region — both /ˈniːðər/ (NEE-ther) and /ˈnaɪðər/ (NYE-ther) are correct. / "Neither" tiene dos pronunciaciones aceptadas según la región — tanto /ˈniːðər/ (NEE-ther) como /ˈnaɪðər/ (NYE-ther) son correctas.

"I like coffee, I don't like tea" (comma splice — missing conjunction)
"I like coffee, but I don't like tea" (comma + coordinating conjunction)
6
Grammar · Comma + FANBOYS rule

When a coordinating conjunction joins two independent (complete) clauses, use a comma before it: "I wanted to go, but it was raining." When joining just two words or phrases, no comma is needed: "tea and coffee."

Cuando una conjunción de coordinación une dos oraciones independientes (completas), usa una coma antes de ella: "I wanted to go, but it was raining." Cuando une solo dos palabras o frases, no se necesita coma: "tea and coffee."
