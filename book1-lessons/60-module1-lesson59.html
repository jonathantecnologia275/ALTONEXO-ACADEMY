Introduction · Introducción

English has two families of possessive words: possessive adjectives (my, your, his) that go before a noun, and possessive pronouns (mine, yours, his) that stand alone. It also has reflexive pronouns (myself, yourself) used when the subject and object are the same person.

El inglés tiene dos familias de palabras posesivas: adjetivos posesivos (my, your, his) que van antes de un sustantivo, y pronombres posesivos (mine, yours, his) que van solos. También tiene pronombres reflexivos (myself, yourself) usados cuando el sujeto y el objeto son la misma persona.

Did you know? / ¿Sabías qué?

"His" is the only word that is both a possessive adjective AND a possessive pronoun with the exact same spelling: "This is his book" (adjective) vs. "This book is his" (pronoun).

"His" es la única palabra que es tanto adjetivo posesivo COMO pronombre posesivo con la misma ortografía exacta: "This is his book" (adjetivo) vs. "This book is his" (pronombre).

"Its" (possessive, no apostrophe) and "it's" (contraction of 'it is') sound identical but are never interchangeable in writing. / "Its" (posesivo, sin apóstrofe) e "it's" (contracción de 'it is') suenan idénticos pero nunca son intercambiables al escribir.

"This book is my" / "I cut myself's finger"
"This book is mine" / "I cut my finger" (don't combine possessive + reflexive incorrectly)
6
Grammar · Possessive adjective vs possessive pronoun vs reflexive

Possessive adjective + noun: "This is my car." Possessive pronoun alone: "This car is mine." Reflexive pronoun when subject=object: "I hurt myself." "She taught herself English."

Adjetivo posesivo + sustantivo: "This is my car." Pronombre posesivo solo: "This car is mine." Pronombre reflexivo cuando sujeto=objeto: "I hurt myself." "She taught herself English."

Tip · Consejo del profesor

Study Tip / Consejo de estudio

When you're unsure whether to use 'my' or 'mine,' check if a noun follows: if yes, use 'my' (my book); if no noun follows, use 'mine' (it's mine).

Cuando no estés seguro si usar 'my' o 'mine,' revisa si sigue un sustantivo: si sí, usa 'my' (my book); si no sigue ningún sustantivo, usa 'mine' (it's mine).
