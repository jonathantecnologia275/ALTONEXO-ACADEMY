Introduction · Introducción

Once you know 1–20, bigger numbers are easy: you just combine a tens word with a ones word, joined by a hyphen.

Una vez que sabes del 1 al 20, los números más grandes son fáciles: solo combinas una palabra de decenas con una de unidades, unidas por un guion.

Did you know? / ¿Sabías qué?

"Twenty-one," "thirty-five," and "ninety-nine" all use a hyphen between the tens and ones words.

"Twenty-one," "thirty-five," y "ninety-nine" usan un guion entre la palabra de decena y la de unidad.

Pronunciation · Pronunciación
forty (not four-ty)
thirty vs thirteen

"Forty" drops the "u" from "four" — it is spelled f-o-r-t-y, not f-o-u-r-t-y. / "Forty" no lleva la "u" de "four" — se escribe f-o-r-t-y, no f-o-u-r-t-y.

"I have thirty and five dollars"
"I have thirty-five dollars"
6
Grammar · Compound numbers

For numbers between 21 and 99 (that are not round tens), join the tens word and the ones word with a hyphen: 21 = twenty-one, 76 = seventy-six.

Para números entre 21 y 99 (que no sean decenas exactas), une la palabra de decena y la de unidad con un guion: 21 = twenty-one, 76 = seventy-six.
