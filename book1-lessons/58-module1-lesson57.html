Introduction · Introducción

Direct speech repeats someone's exact words in quotation marks. Indirect (reported) speech tells what someone said, without quoting them exactly, and usually shifts the tense back.

El discurso directo repite las palabras exactas de alguien entre comillas. El discurso indirecto (reportado) cuenta lo que alguien dijo, sin citarlo exactamente, y generalmente cambia el tiempo verbal hacia atrás.

Did you know? / ¿Sabías qué?

When the reporting verb is in the past ("he said"), the tense in the quote usually shifts back one step — present becomes past, past becomes past perfect.

Cuando el verbo introductorio está en pasado ("he said"), el tiempo en la cita generalmente retrocede un paso — el presente se vuelve pasado, el pasado se vuelve pasado perfecto.

Detailed Explanation · Explicación detallada
Direct: "I am tired"
Directo: "Estoy cansado"
Indirect: He said he was tired
Indirecto: Él dijo que estaba cansado
Direct: "I will call you"
Directo: "Te llamaré"
Indirect: She said she would call me
Indirecto: Ella dijo que me llamaría
Direct: "I have finished"
Directo: "He terminado"
Indirect: He said he had finished
Indirecto: Él dijo que había terminado
Direct: "Are you coming?"
Directo: "¿Vienes?"
Indirect: She asked if I was coming
Indirecto: Ella preguntó si yo venía

Pronunciation · Pronunciación
said /sɛd/
that (often silent/unstressed)

The connector "that" in reported speech ("She said that...") is often optional and unstressed in natural speech. / El conector "that" en el discurso reportado ("She said that...") a menudo es opcional y no se acentúa en el habla natural.

"He said me that he was tired"
"He told me that he was tired" (use 'tell' + person, not 'say' + person)
6
Grammar · Tense backshift rules

Present Simple → Past Simple. Present Continuous → Past Continuous. Present Perfect → Past Perfect. Past Simple → Past Perfect. Will → Would. Can → Could.

Presente Simple → Pasado Simple. Presente Continuo → Pasado Continuo. Presente Perfecto → Pasado Perfecto. Pasado Simple → Pasado Perfecto. Will → Would. Can → Could.
