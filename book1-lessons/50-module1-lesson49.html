Introduction · Introducción

Action verbs let you describe your daily routine step by step — from waking up to going to sleep.

Los verbos de acción te permiten describir tu rutina diaria paso a paso — desde despertarte hasta dormir.

Did you know? / ¿Sabías qué?

In the present simple, verbs don't change for "I, you, we, they" — but add "-s" for "he, she, it": "I wake up" vs. "She wakes up."

En el presente simple, los verbos no cambian para "I, you, we, they" — pero agregan "-s" para "he, she, it": "I wake up" vs. "She wakes up."

Pronunciation · Pronunciación
wakes /weɪks/
studies /ˈstʌdiz/

When adding "-s" for he/she/it, verbs ending in a consonant + "y" change to "-ies": study → studies. / Al agregar "-s" para he/she/it, los verbos que terminan en consonante + "y" cambian a "-ies": study → studies.

"She wake up at six"
"She wakes up at six"
6
Grammar · Present simple: routines

Use present simple for daily routines: "I wake up at six," "He goes to work by bus." Add "-s" or "-es" only for he/she/it.

Usa el presente simple para rutinas diarias: "I wake up at six," "He goes to work by bus." Agrega "-s" o "-es" solo para he/she/it.
