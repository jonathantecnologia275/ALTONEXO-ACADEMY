Introduction · Introducción

Body part vocabulary is essential for describing pain, going to the doctor, and understanding basic health instructions.

El vocabulario de partes del cuerpo es esencial para describir dolor, ir al doctor, y entender instrucciones básicas de salud.

Did you know? / ¿Sabías qué?

In English, "hurt" doesn't change form for the body part — you always say "My X hurts," no matter which part.

En inglés, "hurt" no cambia de forma según la parte del cuerpo — siempre dices "My X hurts," sin importar cuál parte.

"Stomach" doesn't sound like it looks — the "ch" makes a "k" sound: "STUM-uck." / "Stomach" no suena como se escribe — la "ch" hace un sonido de "k": "STUM-uck."

6
Grammar · Expressing pain: hurts

Use "My + body part + hurts" (singular part) or "hurt" (plural part): "My head hurts," "My feet hurt."

Usa "My + parte del cuerpo + hurts" (parte singular) o "hurt" (parte plural): "My head hurts," "My feet hurt."
