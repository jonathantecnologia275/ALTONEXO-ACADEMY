Introduction · Introducción

Weather vocabulary lets you plan your day, choose clothes, and make small talk — one of the most common topics in English.

El vocabulario del clima te permite planear tu día, elegir ropa, y hacer conversación casual — uno de los temas más comunes en inglés.

Did you know? / ¿Sabías qué?

English speakers talk about the weather constantly — it's considered a safe, friendly topic for small talk with strangers.

Los hablantes de inglés hablan del clima constantemente — se considera un tema seguro y amigable para conversación casual con desconocidos.

Pronunciation · Pronunciación
weather /ˈwɛðər/
whether (homophone)

"Weather" and "whether" sound identical but mean different things — context tells you which one is meant. / "Weather" y "whether" suenan idénticos pero significan cosas diferentes — el contexto te dice cuál se quiere decir.

"The weather is very heat today"
"The weather is very hot today"
6
Grammar · IT'S + adjective for weather

Weather is always described with "it's": "It's sunny," "It's raining," "It's very cold." Never say "the weather is have rain."

El clima siempre se describe con "it's": "It's sunny," "It's raining," "It's very cold." Nunca digas "the weather is have rain."
