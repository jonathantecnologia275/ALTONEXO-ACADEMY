Pronunciation · Pronunciación
congratulations
achievement /əˈtʃiːvmənt/

"Congratulations" is always plural — never say "a congratulation." / "Congratulations" siempre es plural — nunca digas "a congratulation."

"I finish the book" (missing time reference)
"I have finished the book" or "I finished the book today"

Grammar · Mixed review: key structures

Review the key patterns from Book 1: "I am a/an + job," "There is/are + place," "I wear/wearing + clothes," "It's + weather," and "My + body part + hurts."

Repasa los patrones clave del Libro 1: "I am a/an + trabajo," "There is/are + lugar," "I wear/wearing + ropa," "It's + clima," y "My + parte del cuerpo + hurts."
